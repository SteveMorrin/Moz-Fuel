🔬 The science, without the food lecture

Protein is set at your requested 190g/day (760 kcal). Fat is set to your chosen percentage of calories. Carbohydrates use the remaining calories at 4 kcal/g; fat uses 9 kcal/g. Those are planning estimates, not an exact physiological requirement.

Your 190g goal at 88.9kg is about 2.14g/kg/day: a little above the often-cited 1.4–2.0g/kg range sufficient for most active adults. Your carb and fat split is a practical preference, not a fixed scientific requirement. Track average weight and training for 2–3 weeks and adjust calories gradually; 2,800 is only a trial starting point. Seek individual advice for any medical concerns.
