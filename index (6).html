🔬 The science, without the food lecture

Daily targets guide your meal choices rather than forcing fractional servings. Protein is fixed at 190g per day (760 kcal); fat follows your selected percentage, with carbohydrate calculated from the remaining calories. Individual meals vary, and nutritional figures are estimates.

At 88.9kg, 190g protein is about 2.14g/kg body weight. Your starting 2,800 kcal is a trial, not a guaranteed surplus; adjust based on your weight trend, strength and appetite. For individual medical or dietary needs, seek a qualified professional.
